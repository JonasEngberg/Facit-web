# Facit — web

The landing page and in-browser deck builder for **Facit**, a flashcard app
that schedules its own reviews and moves decks to your phone with an animated
QR code.

**Live:** <https://facit.study>

## What Facit does

- **Spaced repetition that runs itself.** Every card carries a strength and a
  next-due date; Facit picks the moment to show it to you again.
- **Decks from a table.** Paste a two-column table from ChatGPT, a spreadsheet
  or a web page and the builder fills every card in one click.
- **No account, no upload.** A deck is compressed and looped into an animated
  QR code — point the app at the screen and it reassembles on your phone.
- **Markdown + LaTeX** on both sides of every card.
