Flashcards that schedule themselves. Build a deck from your notes — or a table from ChatGPT — and beam it to your phone with a single animated QR code.

01 · Flashcards

What a flashcard actually does

A flashcard is a prompt and a response: Capital of Sweden on the front, Stockholm on the back. You try to produce the answer from memory before you flip. That small struggle — active recall — is the whole point.

Re-reading a page feels productive and mostly isn't. Pulling the fact back out of your head is what builds a memory that lasts. Facit keeps every card to one clean question so the recall stays honest, and one clean answer so grading yourself is unambiguous.

02 · Timing

Spaced repetition, drawn

A new fact decays on a predictable curve — strong today, shaky in two days, mostly gone in a week. Review it just before it slips, and the curve resets, flatter each time. The next safe gap grows: days, then weeks, then months.

Facit tracks every card's strength and picks that moment for you. Cards you find hard come back tomorrow; cards you know cold get out of your way. You only ever study the handful you're about to lose.

03 · AI decks

From a chat table to a deck, in a minute

You don't have to type a hundred cards. Any assistant that can make a table can make a Facit deck.

  1. Ask for a table

    Prompt ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for a two-column table: term and translation, concept and definition, question and answer. Want multiple choice? Ask for extra columns of wrong answers — or copy a ready-made prompt from the top of the page.

  2. Press Paste from AI

    Copy the table, open the builder, hit Paste from AI. Facit reads Markdown tables, spreadsheet rows and HTML tables from your clipboard and fills every card.

  3. Scan the signal

    The deck is compressed, split and looped into an animated QR code. Point the app at your screen — no file, no login, no typing on glass.

06 · Guide

How to use Facit

Subjects, decks, today's cards, due dates, groups — and how a parent or teacher makes decks for someone else. Short steps, in the words the app uses. Open a topic.

01Subjects and decks

A subject is what you study — Spanish, Biology, History. Each has an emoji and a colour, and holds one or more decks: this week's word list, chapter 4, the test on Friday. Facit plans each subject's cards for you; the decks just keep them in order.

Get a deck into Facit

  • Paste from AI. Ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for a two-column table from your notes, a photo of the page or just a topic. Copy it and tap Paste from AI on the main screen. The prompts on facit.study are ready to copy.
  • Scan QR code. Point Facit at a deck built on facit.study, or at a friend's phone showing a deck's QR code.
  • From group. Add a deck someone shared in one of your groups — see Sharing decks.

Put it in a subject

  1. On Add deck, tap Subject and pick one — or Add new subject….
  2. A new subject gets a title, an emoji and a colour. In a group? Choose which groups see it.
  3. Check the cards, fix anything that's off and tap Done.

Got it in the wrong subject? Open the deck, edit it and pick another subject. Edit deck, card and subject all end with Delete.

02Studying each day

Open Facit and tap a subject. You get today's cards — the ones you're about to forget, and a few new ones.

  • Flip and swipe. Tap to flip. Swipe right if you knew it, left to see it again. A quiz card shows its answers as buttons instead.
  • Up to 20 a day. Each subject gives you at most 20 cards a day, so a big new deck doesn't pile up. Done and want more? Study 20 more — they're extra.
  • New, learning, known. New cards you haven't seen yet. Learning ones come back soon. Known ones wait longer every time you know them. A card you miss starts over.
  • Your streak. Finish today's cards in at least one subject and your streak grows. Days with nothing to study count too. Miss a day and it starts again — your best streak is kept.

The app icon's badge counts the subjects left today, and an afternoon reminder names them. Tap the progress bar while studying to see today's cards.

03Due dates

Studying for something on a set day? Give the deck a due date.

  1. Edit the deck (or build it on facit.study) and turn on Due date.
  2. Pick the Date and a Type: Exam, Test, Homework or Other. Add a Note if you like — it stays on your phone.
  3. The subject shows the date and how ready you are, like “80% ready”. One tap studies every card for it.

Coming up on the main screen lists your due dates — and everyone's in your groups — day by day.

04Groups

A group is friends, a class or a family who see each other's streaks and how far they are with today's cards, subject by subject. Groups need iCloud on each phone; there's no Facit account and no Facit server.

Create a group

  1. Open Groups and tap Add group.
  2. Give it a name and an emoji. You're its admin: only you can add people.
  3. The Invite screen opens with the group's invite code.

Invite people

  1. Show the invite code — tap Invite on the group any time. Keep it open until everyone has scanned.
  2. Each person scans it with the iPhone Camera, or with Scan QR code in Facit, and taps Ask to join. Stand close: the phones talk over Wi-Fi.
  3. They show up on your screen. Add each one, or Add all. For a family, turn on Add without asking and everyone who scans is added.

Join a group

  1. Ask the admin to show the group's invite code.
  2. Scan it with the Camera or Scan QR code, and tap Ask to join.
  3. Once you're added, choose which of your subjects the group sees.

Choose what each group sees

Every group sees your first name, your streak and which days you studied. For each subject you choose, it also sees today's progress, how many cards you know and your due dates with how ready you are — never the cards or your notes.

  • On a subject's page, Groups that see it has a switch for each group.
  • In Group settings, This group sees lists your subjects for that group. See what they see shows you as the group sees you.

Cheer each other on

  • Each member has one button: Cheer once they're done today, Nudge before. Once a day each.
  • A cheer shows on the Groups button and at the top of the group. Turn on notifications for when someone finishes today in Group settings.

Leave a group

Group settings › Leave group. The group stops seeing your stats; your own cards and stats stay. If the admin leaves, the group is deleted for everyone.

05Sharing decks
  • With a QR code. Open a deck's Share deck… and show the QR code. Anyone nearby scans it with Facit and gets their own copy. No group needed.
  • With a group. Long-press a deck and tap Share with group, or say yes when Facit offers it right after you pasted a deck from AI. Everyone in the group is told and can add their own copy for 30 days.
  • Adding one. Tap the notification's Add deck, or From group on the main screen. It opens on Add deck with its subject already picked.

A shared deck carries the cards, title, languages, due date and subject — never your progress or notes. Only you, as the one who shared it, see who added it.

06For parents and teachers

The hard part of studying with cards is making good ones. That's the part you can take off your child's hands — or your students'. They study on their own phone, and you can follow how it goes without seeing their cards or touching their phone.

The quick way: no app for you

  1. Take a photo of the homework, copy a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and attach the photo.
  2. Paste the table it gives you into the deck builder and read through the cards. Set the subject and the test date while you're there.
  3. Your child taps Scan QR code in Facit and points their phone at your screen. The deck is theirs, and nobody needs an account.

That's all it takes. To follow how it goes, and send the next deck without scanning, put Facit on your own iPhone too.

With Facit on your own iPhone

  1. Make a family group and let your child join it — see Groups.
  2. Make a subject for your child. On Add deck, tap Subject › Add new subject…. Under Who studies this?, choose Someone else and type their name. When Facit suggests Link to Elsa in 🏠 Family, tap it — that's what lets you see how they're doing. Next time, their name is already in the list.
  3. Paste the deck and tap Done. Facit asks Share “Rivers of Europe”? with your child already ticked. The deck goes into their own subject, and only they see it and get the notification. Not now is fine too: the share icon next to the deck on the subject's page does the same later.
  4. Follow along. Your subjects for them are under Made for others on your main screen, below For Elsa, with how many of today's cards they've done. Tap their name for their page: their streak, the last few weeks, and Coming up with how ready they are for each date.

What you see

  • On the subject's page, your child's progress in their own copy: today's cards, how many they know, and how ready they are for upcoming dates. Did they already have the subject under another name? Tap Not Elsa's subject? Pick another.
  • Next to each deck you shared, whether they've added it yet.
  • Your child chooses which subjects the group sees. If one is missing, they turn it on under Groups that see it on that subject's page.
  • Add photo on their page puts a picture from your Contacts next to their name. It stays on your phone and is never sent to the group.
  • Subjects you make for someone else never count in your own streak, stats or groups. Studying one is only trying it out.

For teachers

Make a group for the class and share decks with the whole group. You see who has added each one — “added by Elsa and Leo · 2 of 3” — and everyone's test dates in Coming up. To follow one student on their own, make a subject for them; two children doing Spanish get one each.

You never see your child's notes or how they did on each card, and you can't change anything on their phone. Their studying stays theirs.

07Quiz games
  1. Long-press a deck with at least three quiz cards and tap Play quiz game.
  2. Everyone in the room scans the code with Facit and joins with their name — up to 8 players. Just have Wi-Fi switched on.
  3. 20 seconds a question. Places are revealed from last to first. Afterwards, Facit can make a group with the players.

A quiz card is a card with wrong answers. Ask an AI for extra columns of wrong answers, or add them to a card yourself. A game never changes anyone's study schedule.

08Your stats

Tap your streak, or Your stats on the main screen: your day streak and best streak, the last 12 weeks, today by subject and the due dates coming up. How Facit works, next to it, has the short version of this page.

08 · Questions

Before you download

Is Facit free?

Downloading Facit and importing decks from a QR code is free.

Do I need an account?

No. The animated QR code carries the entire deck, so there's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sync.

Can my group see my cards?

No. A group sees your first name, your streak and which days you studied. For the subjects you choose to share, it sees each subject's title, colour, today's progress, how many cards you know and your due dates with how ready you are. Never the cards or your notes. If you share a deck with the group, everyone in it can add their own copy for 30 days — the cards, title, languages, due date and subject, but never your progress or notes.

What do I need for groups?

Facit on each phone, signed in to iCloud with iCloud on for Facit. A group lives in its admin's iCloud — there's no Facit account and no Facit server. Leave a group any time: it stops seeing your stats, and your own cards and stats stay. If the admin leaves, the group is deleted for everyone.

Which AI tools work with Paste from AI?

Any that can output a table — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — or a plain spreadsheet. Two columns make flashcards; further columns are wrong answers for quiz cards. Facit parses the table, not the tool.

Can I make quiz cards?

Yes. Add wrong answers to a card and Facit shows the correct answer and the wrong ones as buttons in random order — pick right and the card counts as known, pick wrong and it comes back to repeat. From an AI, ask for a table like: Question | Correct answer | Wrong answer 1 | Wrong answer 2 | Wrong answer 3. Column 2 is always the correct answer. Flashcards and quiz cards can share a deck. Older versions of the app import quiz cards as ordinary flashcards.

How large can a deck be?

There's no fixed limit. Bigger decks simply add QR frames and take a few seconds longer to scan.

Can cards use Markdown or math?

Yes. Card text takes Markdown for emphasis, lists and code, and LaTeX for formulas — write $E=mc^2$ inline or $$…$$ for a centred block. The builder shows every card rendered; click a question or answer to edit it.

Where does my data go?

The builder runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; the QR code is generated locally on this page.
