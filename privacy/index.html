Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Facit does not collect any personal data.

Your flashcard decks are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to your other devices, in your private database. We have no access to that data.

Facit has no user accounts, contains no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking, and includes no third-party SDKs. The camera is used only, on your request, to scan a deck QR code; camera frames are processed on-device and never stored or transmitted.
